# Storyboard

This explains how the site tells the story, why each chapter is there, and what it needs to stay
accurate. Use it when you add a chapter or rewrite one.

## The idea

Elio's first big win was the Solana *Speedrun* Game Jam, and his career really is a sequence of
timed segments. So the site borrows the speedrunner's split timer: the journey is a run, each chapter
is a split, and the timer on the left shows where the reader is. A gold diamond marks a split with a
1st-place finish, which is what "gold split" means to speedrunners: a best segment.

One chapter, the Cryptara Conquest launch, is the climax. It's the only dark, full-width section,
and the only place numbers count up.

## Who reads it, and what each of them needs

| Reader | What they need | Where they find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter, 30 seconds | Role, location, what he's done, CV | Header: intro, Now/Before/Based in, **Download CV** |
| Hiring engineer | Evidence of hard technical work | *Mobile and open source* (SDK, 94% bug, verification), *Terrace* |
| Investor | Traction, funding history, founder track record | *The studio*, *Winning*, *Launch*, *Terrace* numbers |
| Collaborator or community | What he's built and where he shows up | *Everything I've shipped*, *Around the work* |

## Writing rules

- First person, plain sentences, past tense for the past.
- Every number comes from the CV. Don't round up, and keep the time window with the number
  ("in its first six weeks").
- Say "we" for studio work and "I" for what Elio did himself.
- No marketing phrases. Describe what happened.

## Facts to confirm before launch

These appear on the site, and the CV doesn't pin them down:

1. **Bonkathon year.** The *Winning* chapter avoids giving a year for the Bonkathon win. Add it if you
   want it shown.
2. **Chainlink start year.** The CV says 2021 in one place and 2022 in another, so the site only
   mentions the current Developer Expert role.
3. **Seeker numbers.** 1,000+ players, 13,000+ hours and 367K+ transactions: up to what date?
4. **The 94% bug.** 94% of how many accounts? Readers may ask.
